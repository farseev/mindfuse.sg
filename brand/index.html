Logo

Two curved strokes meet and fuse into a single loop, the meeting of AI reading and human judgement, and of the two sides of every tension. Download: logo.svg · logo-dark.svg · mark.svg

  • Keep clear space equal to the height of the mark on every side.
  • Minimum size: 24 px tall on screen. Use the mark alone below 120 px wide.
  • Never recolour, stretch, outline or add effects. Use the dark version on #2B1640 and photography.
  • Pair with the SOMIN partner lockup only as “Technology partner SOMIN”, never merged into the Mindfuse logo.

Colour

Accent is for actions, highlights and data. Body text on light surfaces uses #3E2E4D; links use #B83A28 to keep AA contrast.

Section label

Find the tensions that move Singapore and SEA audiences

Mindfuse is a consumer-psychology and cultural-insight consultancy. We decode personas, tensions and cultural codes from real conversations, then turn them into briefs your teams can act on.

Personality

  • perceptive
  • warm
  • candid
  • respectful

Voice: do

  • Name tensions in plain words
  • Show the evidence behind each claim
  • Write about cultures with specificity and care

Voice: don't

  • Use ethnicity as a shortcut
  • Mock cultural traits such as kiasu
  • Hype AI or promise magic insight

Imagery

Editorial illustration in coral and warm orange on deep plum, with fine connected-line motifs and Singapore botanical and urban cues. Abstract figures only, no identifiable people.

All imagery is generated with SOMIN image generation from art-directed prompts: no stock, no text baked into images, no identifiable real people.
