# Mindfuse brand guidelines (v1.0, October 2026)

**Positioning:** Mindfuse turns real SEA conversations into the personas and tensions that explain why people choose, with cultural care.
**Tagline:** Consumer psychology and culture, decoded for SEA
**Domain / contact:** mindfuse.sg · ask@mindfuse.sg
**Technology partner:** SOMIN (somin.ai). Show as "Technology partner SOMIN" with the two-tone SOMIN wordmark (SO teal #2DBF9C, MIN dark/white), never merged into our logo.

## Logo
Two curved strokes meet and fuse into a single loop, the meeting of AI reading and human judgement, and of the two sides of every tension.
Files: `brand/logo.svg` (light backgrounds), `brand/logo-dark.svg` (dark backgrounds), `brand/mark.svg` (icon), `favicon.svg`.
Clear space = height of the mark. Minimum 24 px tall. No recolouring, stretching, outlines or effects.

## Typography
- Headlines: Fraunces 600, tracking -0.02em, sentence case
- Body: DM Sans 400/500, 17 px / 1.65
- Labels: DM Sans 600, 12–13 px, uppercase, 0.2em tracking, accent colour
- Corner radius: 22px

## Personality
- perceptive
- warm
- candid
- respectful

## Voice
Do:
- Name tensions in plain words
- Show the evidence behind each claim
- Write about cultures with specificity and care

Don't:
- Use ethnicity as a shortcut
- Mock cultural traits such as kiasu
- Hype AI or promise magic insight

## Imagery
Editorial illustration in coral and warm orange on deep plum, with fine connected-line motifs and Singapore botanical and urban cues. Abstract figures only, no identifiable people. All imagery generated with SOMIN image generation; no text in images, no stock, no identifiable real people.

## Calls to action
Every primary CTA ("Start a tension study") opens an email to ask@mindfuse.sg.
